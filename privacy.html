Privacy policy: SpaceBites uploader

Last updated: 2026-11-21

SpaceBites uploader is a private tool used only by the SpaceBites channel to publish its own videos. This page explains what it does with Google user data.

What it accesses

  • Permission to upload videos to the SpaceBites YouTube channel (YouTube scope youtube.upload).
  • Read-only access to that channel (youtube.readonly), to check that an upload went public and to read the view and like counts of its own videos.

How it uses that data

Only to upload SpaceBites videos at their planned time and to see how they perform. The view and like counts go into a private summary that the channel's video-planning assistant (Claude, by Anthropic) reads to choose future topics. The tool doesn't access anyone else's account, collects nothing about viewers, and shows no ads.

Storage and sharing

The login is stored only on the channel owner's private computer and home server. Google user data isn't sold, isn't shared with anyone except as described above, and isn't used to train AI models. The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube

SpaceBites uploader uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time in your Google Account under Third-party connections; the stored login then stops working.

Contact

Questions about this policy: write to the contact address shown on Google's sign-in screen for SpaceBites uploader, or through the SpaceBites YouTube channel.
